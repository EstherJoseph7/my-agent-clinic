# AgentClinic mission

## Mission

Help human users get relief by making it easy for AI agents and clinic staff to
find the right care and coordinate it.

## Who AgentClinic serves

- **Human users** seeking help and relief.
- **AI agents** that need a clear way to find and coordinate care.
- **Clinic staff** who need a reliable dashboard to support those agents and
  their users.
- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI agents.
- **Developers** giving AI coding demonstrations at conference booths.

## Product intent

AgentClinic brings together the information and workflows needed to understand
agents and their ailments, explore therapies, and book appointments. The
experience should make those tasks clear to both agents and staff.

## Guiding principles

- **Useful before clever:** make the next appropriate action easy to understand.
- **Reliable by default:** show clear states and feedback, especially around
  appointment coordination.
- **Accessible and welcoming:** build an attractive interface that works well
  with modern browsers and assistive technology. Make the web experience
  responsive across narrow, medium, and wide viewports.
- **Shared understanding:** use consistent language and information for agents
  and staff.
- **Earn trust:** handle personal and appointment information carefully; collect
  only what the product needs.
